### T057 — Benchmark Interactive GPU And Resource Paths

#### Objective

Measure the final real workbench gesture/settle, resident scatter/timeline,
readback, relief/transition, and concurrent RAM/VRAM/upload generation paths on
named hardware/backends before performance/resource claims or defaults.

#### Scope

- Primary audit finding: RS-134.
- Extend T033's versioned benchmark metadata/RAII support with real coordinator
  gesture submission, preview, exact settle, and stale/cancel scenarios.
- Benchmark resident scatter/timeline no-readback/max/full-readback operations and
  relief quality precompute/presentation resources.
- Record p50/p95 event-thread submission and exact completion separately.
- Record accounted RAM/VRAM, uploads, staging, live/retiring generations/tickets,
  and optional clearly labelled process/driver metrics.
- Require dataset/config/command/commit/date/OS/CPU/RAM/GPU adapter/backend/driver.

#### Out of Scope

- Do not benchmark isolated helpers the product bypasses.
- Do not publish README claims/change defaults automatically.
- Do not require external flagship data in CI or call accounted bytes resident.
- Do not optimize within this measurement task.

#### Blocked By

T024, T025, T026, T028, T029, T030, T033.

#### Why It Is Blocked

Interactive measurement needs final engines/coordinator/controllers/budgets and
the reproducible metadata/fixture framework.

#### Implementation Details

Scenarios name dataset rows/shape, grid, filters, gesture count, preview/exact
policy, readback, relief tier, and live-generation budget. Event submission samples
measure handler-to-return; exact completion measures request-to-published generation
and identifies CPU/GPU waiting. GPU runs record adapter/backend/driver.

Resource metrics use owned `ResourceReport`; optional RSS/driver metrics are labelled
estimated/observed. Retain raw summary metadata sufficient to compute p50/p95, not
huge traces.

- Wrong: compile means measured. Correct: contextual run only.
- Wrong: benchmark submit but call it settle. Correct: separate metrics.
- Wrong: compare unlike GPUs without disclosure. Correct: hardware-bound results.

- Submission/completion/resource metrics are distinct.
- Real production seams are invoked.
- No claim/default change without human review.
